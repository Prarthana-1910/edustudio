# Life-Cycle Decarbonization: LC3 and Alkali-Activated Geopolymers in Massive Foundations

**Field Investigation Log:** SEC MAT-02 / STA 00+040  
**Research Lead:** Materials Characterization Lab with Thornton Tomasetti Sustainability  
**Embodied Carbon Delta:** -48.6% kg CO2e / m3  

## Introduction
Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC) production accounts for roughly 8% of anthropogenic greenhouse gas emissions. For thick structural raft foundations—such as those supporting 60-story residential towers—hydration heat build-up further compounds the durability risks through delayed ettringite formation (DEF) and thermal cracking.

EduStudio partnered with regional batching plants and consulting engineers to validate a 65% supplementary cementitious material (SCM) ternary blend utilizing Limestone Calcined Clay Cement (LC3) and Ground Granulated Blast-Furnace Slag (GGBS).

## 1. Mix Formulation Matrix (per cubic meter)
- **Binder Component 1:** CEM I 52.5R (OPC) = $140\text{ kg}$
- **Binder Component 2:** Calcined Metakaolin Clay = $110\text{ kg}$
- **Binder Component 3:** Ground Calcium Carbonate (Limestone) = $60\text{ kg}$
- **Binder Component 4:** GGBS Grade 100 = $130\text{ kg}$
- **Water/Binder Ratio:** 0.36
- **Superplasticizer (PCE):** 1.2% by binder mass
- **Target Slump Flow:** 650mm (Self-Consolidating Concrete / SCC)

## 2. Adiabatic Calorimetry & Heat of Hydration
Semi-adiabatic calorimetry cells monitored temperature rise over 168 hours in a 2.4-meter mock raft cube:
- **Peak Core Temperature:** $46.8^\circ\text{C}$ in the LC3-GGBS blend versus $71.2^\circ\text{C}$ recorded in the baseline 100% OPC control block.
- **Differential Temperature Limit:** Core-to-surface gradient remained below $16^\circ\text{C}$, comfortably within the strict $20^\circ\text{C}$ Eurocode thermal restraint safety limit without requiring internal cooling coils.

## 3. Compressive Strength Development
| Curing Age | Control OPC (MPa) | LC3-GGBS Blend (MPa) | Specification Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **3 Days** | 28.4 | 19.8 | > 15.0 MPa |
| **7 Days** | 41.2 | 34.6 | > 28.0 MPa |
| **28 Days** | 52.0 | 54.8 | > 45.0 MPa |
| **90 Days** | 56.1 | 67.2 | > 50.0 MPa |

*Noticeable secondary pozzolanic reaction between calcined kaolinite and free calcium hydroxide between days 14 and 90 leads to pronounced pore refinement and superior ultimate compressive strength.*

## 4. Rapid Chloride Permeability Test (RCPT)
At 56 days, total charge passed dropped to **412 Coulombs** (classified as "Very Low Permeability" per ASTM C1202), compared to 2,450 Coulombs for ordinary Portland cement, underscoring high resistance to marine de-icing salt ingress.
