# Diaphragm Wall Lateral Deflection & Ground Settlement in Deep Soft Clay Excavations

**Field Investigation Log:** SEC GEO-03 / STA 04+110  
**Research Lead:** Geotechnical Engineering Division with Arup Geotechnics  
**Status:** Monitored Multi-Stage Dewatering & Top-Down Construction  

## Executive Summary
Excavations exceeding 24 meters in depth adjacent to historic masonry structures impose stringent boundary deformation constraints. In this site investigation conducted at Station 04+110, an 800mm thick reinforced concrete diaphragm wall with four levels of pre-stressed ground anchors was instrumented with in-place biaxial inclinometers, vibrating wire piezometers, and automated total station prism networks.

## 1. Subsurface Stratigraphy & Soil Mechanics
The geotechnical baseline boring program revealed three distinct strata:
1. **Fill & Alluvium (0 to 4.5m):** Silty sand with SPT N-values ranging between 8 and 14.
2. **Soft-to-Medium Marine Clay (4.5 to 19.8m):** Undrained shear strength $s_u = 28 + 1.8z\text{ kPa}$, void ratio $e_0 = 1.15$, compression index $C_c = 0.42$. High sensitivity ($S_t \approx 4$).
3. **Dense Glacial Till (> 19.8m):** Dense gravelly sand with SPT $N > 50$, friction angle $\phi' = 38^\circ$.

## 2. Wall Deflection vs. Numerical Plaxis 2D Hardening Soil Predictions
Maximum lateral displacement occurred during Excavation Stage 3 (depth 18.5m) prior to the activation of Anchor Tier 4:
- **Observed Inclinometer Peak:** $\delta_{h,\max} = 31.4\text{ mm}$ (located at depth 13.2m).
- **Ratio to Excavation Depth:** $\delta_{h,\max} / H = 0.17\%$, well within the Clough & O'Rourke empirical threshold ($0.2\%$) for braced walls in clay.
- **Surface Settlement Basin:** Maximum settlement was logged at an offset distance of $d = 0.85H$ from the wall face, with an absolute settlement magnitude of $18.2\text{ mm}$.

## 3. Groundwater Hydrodynamics
Vibrating wire piezometers stationed outside the perimeter wall revealed negligible drawdown in the upper unconfined aquifer during vacuum dewatering inside the excavation envelope, confirming the watertightness of the CWS (Continuous Water Stop) stop-end joint details between primary and secondary panels.

## 4. Corporate Practice Implication
Junior engineers and students shadowing this project observed that anchor pre-stress lock-off loads must be calibrated dynamically following each bench removal cycle. Staged relaxation in soft clays can trigger sudden redistribution into lower anchor tiers if thermal expansion of steel waler beams is not accounted for during summer daylight exposure.
